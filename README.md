# InfoViz-PowerBI-Tableau
This stores the files resulting from my research Information Visualization for my MSIS degree in AI at University of Arkansas - Litlle Rock (UALR).  The main use case/problem was to help someone moving to another location (due to a new job, e.g.) find the best place to live near the new workplace.  This is very relevant as we are a very mobile workforce -- United Van Lines 2021 study showed that over 30% of the moves in their network was due to job changes.

In this repository, you will find:
1. A video describing the problem/use case, the methodology applied, the information visualization techniques analyzed (ArcGIS versus choropleth maps, hover-over focus, data aggregation versus simplification versus details), and the analysis and choice of the visualization tool (Power BI versus Tableau)
2. The presentation supporting the video analysis above
3. The datasets used to create the selected information visualization approach
4. PowerBI visualization
5. Tableau visualization
